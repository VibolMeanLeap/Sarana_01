POS layout integration (one additive include)
==============================================

Open: resources/views/layout/pos.blade.php

Find the existing POS navbar action group. In the supplied project it begins:

<div class="d-flex align-items-center flex-wrap gap-2 gap-md-3">

Immediately inside that div, BEFORE the staff-name span, add:

@include('partials.pos-language-settings')

For example:

<div class="d-flex align-items-center flex-wrap gap-2 gap-md-3">
    @include('partials.pos-language-settings')
    <span class="small text-muted d-none d-sm-inline">
        <i class="bi bi-person-circle me-1"></i>{{ auth()->user()->name ?? 'Staff' }}
    </span>
    ...keep your existing Workspace, Orders, and Sign out controls...
</div>

Do not replace the whole POS layout. Your live version may contain later COD,
AJAX, notification, and payment improvements that must remain intact.

The partial uses the existing route name `language.switch` and the existing
translator asset `public/assets/js/cc-khmer-ui.js` and dictionary
`public/assets/lang/km-ui.json` from the bilingual localization patch.
