Optional: show the same language preference in Admin > Settings
===============================================================

Open: resources/views/admin/meanleap/setting.blade.php

Find the existing line:
@include('admin.message.message')

Immediately AFTER it, add:

@include('admin.partials.language-preferences-card')

This adds a Language & Display card to the Admin Settings page. It is optional;
the POS Settings dropdown works independently and is the part needed by cashiers.
